Approach

A design partner, not a consultant-for-hire.

Three Tomorrows works the way the best in-house teams work. Anchored in your context. Built alongside your people. Accountable to outcomes you can measure.

What "design partner" means.

Most consultants sell distance. They come in, run discovery, build a deck, present it, leave. Six months later the deck sits in a SharePoint folder and nothing has changed.

A design partner sells proximity. Three Tomorrows sits inside the work with your team. We bring the AI domain depth, the build skills and the operator instincts. Your team brings the context, the constraints and the ownership. What gets built is co-owned. What gets shipped, ships because the team built it.

It's the only adoption pattern that durably sticks.

The four-step method.

Discover · Co-Design · Pilot · Scale
01 · Discover

Map where AI earns its keep.

We start by mapping where AI can earn its keep in your business. Honest prioritisation, no slideware. We interview the team, audit the work, identify the use cases worth the time.

Output: a ranked roadmap with the top 3 to 5 candidates and the rationale for each.
02 · Co-Design

Build with the team.

We build the framework, the content, and the workflows alongside the team who'll use them. Workshops two to three times a week. Real artefacts, not deliverables.

Output: working prototypes, not slides.
03 · Pilot

Run real with real users.

We run the work with a willing cohort or business unit. Capture what works. Refine the rest. The pilot is real, in production, with real users.

Output: a shipped workflow with measurable use.
04 · Scale

Productise what stuck.

We productise what stuck. The team owns it. We're around for the next horizon, not the next invoice.

Output: a playbook your team can use to repeat the pattern.

What we won't do.

We won't write a strategy deck and walk away. We won't promise transformations we can't measure. We won't recommend a vendor we don't believe in. We won't take on engagements where Three Tomorrows isn't the right partner. If we're not, we'll say so and try to point you somewhere useful.
