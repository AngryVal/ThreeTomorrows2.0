About

I'm Valerio Veo.
People call me Val.

I've spent the last twenty years building digital products and businesses inside Australia's biggest media, sport and technology companies. Three Tomorrows is what I do now.

The career

Senior digital and product roles at Disney, ESPN, the AFL, Optus, Ten Digital, SBS, ABC and ninemsn. I led the teams that ran Footytips at 1.3 million players. I architected CRM systems that managed 6.5 million records. I shipped products used by 3 million+ monthly users. I sat in the rooms where digital transformations actually happened, not just the ones where they were talked about.

Why Three Tomorrows

I started Three Tomorrows because I lived the problem from the other side. I watched too many large organisations spend too much money on consultants who couldn't build. I watched too many ambitious teams stall on AI initiatives because nobody in the room had actually shipped one before.

Three Tomorrows is the practice I wish had existed when I was a CEO. One senior operator, plugged into your team, building what gets shipped.

Why AI

AI is the most important technology shift since the smartphone, and most Australian businesses are nowhere near ready for it. Not because they're slow. Because the consulting market is full of people selling theory. I want to help Australian leaders move past the slideware and start building.

What I believe

AI strategy is meaningless if nothing ships.

Adoption is the metric, not the strategy deck.

The leader is the variable, not the model.

Same tools, different outcomes. The difference is in the room.

Co-design is the only adoption pattern that sticks.

AI built by the team gets used. AI delivered to the team gets resented.

Hype cycle and underlying shift can both be real.

You don't have to choose between cynicism and FOMO.

Australian businesses don't need to copy Silicon Valley.

They need to build what works inside their own context.
