Service 01 · Embedded

Fractional AI & Digital Lead.

Senior AI and digital leadership embedded with your team, two to three days a week. For founder-led teams, SMEs and not-for-profits that need an experienced operator without the cost of a full-time hire.

Who this is for.

CEOs and MDs of $10M to $100M Australian businesses who know AI matters but don't yet have the senior leader who can run it. Founder-led teams whose first hire shouldn't be a $400K CTO. Not-for-profits and member organisations balancing limited budgets against rising digital expectations. Boards needing an experienced voice in the room while the permanent shape gets figured out.

What you get.

A senior leader plugged into the exec team two to three days a week. Strategy and execution sitting in the same chair. The mandate runs across the AI agenda, the digital roadmap, vendor selection, hiring decisions, and the operating rhythm that makes the whole thing land.

In practice that means setting the AI strategy and committing to a 90-day cadence; sitting in board and exec meetings as the AI and digital voice; running team workshops to build literacy and confidence; selecting vendors and saying no when the pitch is wrong; designing the operating rhythm that lets the team ship; and mentoring the internal people who'll eventually take it on.

How it runs.

Engagements run for three to six months minimum. Two to three days a week, on site and remote in whatever mix suits the business. Daily Slack or Teams presence. Weekly exec touchpoint. Monthly board update. Quarterly review against outcomes agreed at the outset.

Sample outcomes.

  • A 90-day AI roadmap your team can ship against, with the first two workflows in production
  • A senior team that can read a vendor pitch and tell the noise from the signal
  • The internal AI or digital lead recruited, onboarded, and set up to succeed
  • An AI literacy baseline lifted across the leadership group

FAQ.

Four questions we get asked most
How is this different from hiring a Big 4 consultant?

A Big 4 partner runs the slides, the associate runs the work. Three Tomorrows is one operator with twenty years of senior experience, in the room with your team, building what gets shipped.

Do you replace our internal AI lead, or help us hire one?

Both options. Some clients use the fractional model as a bridge to a permanent hire. Others use it because a permanent hire isn't the right move for their size and stage.

What size businesses suit this?

Best fit is $10M to $100M revenue, 50 to 500 employees, with an existing leadership team. Outside that range we'll be candid about whether we're the right partner.

Will you write code and build things?

Yes, when it's the right move. The fractional engagement is led by strategy, but Three Tomorrows builds and ships AI workflows where it makes sense. See AI Workflows & Solutions.
