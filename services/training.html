Service 03 · Learn

AI Leadership Training.

Mindset, skillset and toolset programs for executive teams, boards and senior cohorts. The literacy a leader actually needs to make AI decisions and lead the change.

Who this is for.

Executive teams who need to make real AI decisions in the next twelve months. Boards forming a view on AI risk, capability and investment. Senior leadership cohorts inside larger organisations preparing for AI-augmented operations. L&D and leadership development partners who own the customer relationship and want a substantive AI co-design partner.

The framework.

Mindset · Skillset · Toolset

A program structured around three pillars. The literacy comes in that order, and the program does too.

01

Mindset

How a leader holds AI before they touch a tool. The model is not the advantage; the leader is. Adoption is not acceptance. The Gen Z resentment building inside organisations is a leadership problem, not a comms problem.

02

Skillset

The practical capability a leader needs personally. Critical evaluation of AI output. Prompt and context as a thinking discipline. Knowing when not to use AI. Leading workflow redesign. Running the four-condition diagnostic on their own business unit.

03

Toolset

Pragmatic literacy across the current landscape. LLMs, agents, vertical AI, orchestration. How to evaluate vendor pitches without being captured. How to build a personal and team workflow that compounds. The tokenomics lens coming through in 2026.

How it runs.

Three formats · One framework
01 · Half-day session

Executive or board

3 to 4 hours · Single cohort

Mindset-led, lightly into Skillset. Best as a starting point or an annual reset.

02 · Full-day program

Senior leadership cohort

1 day · Single cohort

The full Mindset, Skillset, Toolset arc. Best for a senior leadership cohort inside one organisation.

03 · Multi-cohort program

Organisation-wide rollout

3 to 4 days · Across weeks or months

With practical assignments between sessions. Best for rolling AI literacy across a senior population.
