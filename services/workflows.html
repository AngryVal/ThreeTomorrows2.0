Service 04 · Build

AI Workflows & Solutions.

Custom agentic workflows, GEO strategy, and the systems that turn AI strategy into operational advantage. The build, not the pitch.

Who this is for.

Teams who've identified the use case and need it built. Senior leaders wanting personal productivity systems that match how they actually work. Marketing teams whose audience is shifting from Google search to AI answers. Operations and customer service teams ready to put agentic workflows into production.

What you get.

Three things, depending on the brief
01

Agentic AI workflows

Custom-built workflows on the Claude ecosystem (Claude Cowork, Claude Code, MCP) plus your existing tools. Designed to handle the dull, the difficult and the repetitive without putting humans out of the loop.

02

GEO strategy

Generative Engine Optimisation. Strategy and implementation for organisations losing visibility as discovery shifts from search to AI answers. Delivered in partnership with Norg.ai, the Australian GEO platform Three Tomorrows partners with.

03

CEO AI OS

A personalised agentic AI system for senior leaders, running through Claude Cowork. Connects email, calendar, meeting transcripts and files through a Chief-of-Staff agent that knows how you work. Currently in closed beta.

How it runs.

Three engagement shapes
01 · Discovery and Build

Scope, design, ship

4 to 8 weeks · Fixed scope

Scope, design, build, ship one or two specific workflows. Best when you know the use case.

02 · Productisation

Pattern across teams

8 to 12 weeks · Multi-team rollout

For organisations rolling a pattern across multiple teams. We build the first instance, document the pattern, equip your team to replicate.

03 · Embedded build

Inside a Fractional engagement

Ongoing

For Fractional AI & Digital Lead engagements, build sits inside the broader retainer.

FAQ.

Four questions we get asked most
Are you a dev shop?

No. Three Tomorrows is a senior-led practice that builds where building is the right move. For large-scale engineering projects we'll be candid about whether we're the right partner.

What tech do you build on?

Claude ecosystem first. MCP for connectors. Your existing tools wherever possible. No proprietary platform you have to keep paying for.

How does GEO work in practice?

We assess where your brand currently shows up in AI-generated answers, identify the gaps, and run a structured program to lift citation share. Run with Norg.ai as the underlying platform.
